## Current enabling-infrastructure evidence layers

- **BEAD** — Approved final proposal. Texas says its BEAD Final Proposal was approved Dec. 4, 2025. The BDO selected 22 applicants to serve more than 240,000 broadband-serviceable locations and more than 2,700 community anchor institutions, with about $1.2B in federal awards and $177M in state match. [Source](https://comptroller.texas.gov/programs/broadband/funding/bead/)
- **BOOT** — Projects in delivery. Texas reports BOOT I awards of $10.4M covering 1,468 residential locations, 77 businesses and 15 community anchor institutions; BOOT I projects are due by Dec. 31, 2026. BOOT II reports $623.4M awarded for 61,185 residential locations, 18,250 businesses and 2,191 community anchor institutions, with project status published by the BDO. [Source](https://comptroller.texas.gov/programs/broadband/funding/boot/)
- **FCC map / Fabric** — Continuously updated federal dataset. The FCC National Broadband Map shows provider-reported availability and allows location and availability challenges. FCC guidance explicitly distinguishes availability from real-world performance, affordability and adoption. [Source](https://help.bdc.fcc.gov/hc/en-us/articles/10467446103579-How-to-Use-the-FCC-s-National-Broadband-Map)
- **Census** — 2024 ACS 1-year Texas estimate. ACS table B28003 reports 11,449,769 Texas households in the 2024 estimate; 10,641,140 had a broadband internet subscription, 485,603 had a computer but no internet subscription, and 317,475 had no computer. [Source](https://data.census.gov/table/ACSDT1Y2024.B28003?g=040XX00US48)
- **E-Rate** — FY2026 program guidance available. USAC describes E-Rate as discounts for eligible schools and libraries on internet/data transmission and eligible internal connections. Funding-year eligibility changes, so applicants must use the current Eligible Services List and process guidance. [Source](https://www.usac.org/e-rate/)
- **Libraries** — Texas library broadband support active. TSLAC reports library broadband/E-Rate support and 2026 accreditation standards. Commission minutes from Aug. 1, 2025 reported Libraries Connecting Texas supporting 169 library systems, with more than $1.35M in discount funding requests and an average discount of 83%. [Source](https://www.tsl.texas.gov/ldn/accreditation)
- **Telehealth** — SFY2026 rural tele-connectivity grant cycle documented. Texas HHSC released the SFY2026 Pediatric Tele-Connectivity Resource Program for Rural Texas to connect eligible rural hospitals and rural health clinics with pediatric specialists/subspecialists or participating higher-education institutions. [Source](https://resources.hhs.texas.gov/rfa/hhs0017228)
- **Workforce** — 2026 digital-skills guidance. Texas Workforce Commission guidance updated in 2026 treats digital-skills building as an allowable workforce-development activity and explicitly integrates AI literacy. TWC also operates training programs such as Skills for Success and regional workforce programs. [Source](https://www.twc.texas.gov/sites/default/files/wf/policy-letter/wd/20-21-ch2-twc.pdf)
- **Library infrastructure / public access** — FY2026 Texas grant guidance published. TSLAC describes its LIFI grant program as supporting broadband infrastructure and facility improvements in eligible public libraries, including projects aimed at at least 100/100 Mbps and spaces that can support education, work and health monitoring. [Source](https://www.tsl.texas.gov/ldn/grants/programs)
